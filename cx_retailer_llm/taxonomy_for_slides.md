# CX Contact Reason Taxonomy

## Overview

- **6** Top-Level Categories
- **48** Subcategories
- Built from **36,842** contact transcripts

---

## Order Status and Delivery Issues

**Covers inquiries related to order status, delivery delays, missing or incorrect deliveries, and related updates.**

### Subcategories:

- **Order Status Inquiries**: Covers requests for updates on the status of an order, including whether it has been assigned, picked, or delivered.
- **Delivery Delays**: Covers issues related to delayed deliveries, including inquiries about reasons for delays and requests for updated delivery times.
- **Missing or Incorrect Deliveries**: Covers reports of orders not being delivered, delivered to the wrong address, or containing missing or incorrect items.
- **Order Cancellations**: Covers requests to cancel orders for various reasons, including customer requests, out-of-stock items, or system issues.
- **Refunds and Compensation**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to missing items, incorrect orders, or delivery issues.
- **Technical or App Issues**: Covers issues with app functionality, such as errors during checkout, payment declines, or device malfunctions affecting order processing.
- **Shopper or Driver Behavior**: Covers complaints or concerns about shopper or driver behavior, including unprofessional conduct, communication issues, or policy violations.
- **Order Modifications**: Covers requests to modify orders, such as adding items, changing delivery times, or updating customer information.

## Order Modifications and Cancellations

**Covers requests to modify or cancel orders due to customer changes, unavailable items, or system issues.**

### Subcategories:

- **Duplicate Order Cancellations**: Requests to cancel duplicate orders placed in error or due to system glitches.
- **Unavailable Items Cancellations**: Requests to cancel orders due to unavailable items or dissatisfaction with substitutions.
- **Wrong Address Deliveries**: Issues related to orders delivered to the wrong address, including requests for redelivery, refunds, or resolution.
- **Customer-Initiated Cancellations**: Cancellations requested directly by customers for personal reasons, such as changes in plans or errors in the order.
- **System or Technical Issues**: Cancellations or modifications due to system errors, technical glitches, or app malfunctions.
- **Payment and Billing Issues**: Cancellations or modifications related to payment failures, incorrect payment methods, or billing concerns.
- **Shopper or Driver Issues**: Cancellations or modifications due to issues with shoppers or delivery drivers, such as unprofessional behavior or inability to fulfill orders.
- **Order Status and Verification**: Inquiries about the status of cancellations, confirmations, or reasons for cancellations.

## Refunds and Compensation

**Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to missing items, incorrect orders, damaged goods, or service failures.**

### Subcategories:

- **Missing Items**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to items missing from an order, whether partially or entirely.
- **Incorrect Items**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to receiving items that were not ordered or incorrect substitutions.
- **Damaged Items**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to receiving damaged, spoiled, or unusable items.
- **Delivery Issues**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to issues with delivery, such as late deliveries, misdeliveries, or items left in unsuitable conditions.
- **Order Cancellations**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation related to order cancellations, including cancellations due to missing items or customer dissatisfaction.
- **Shopper or Driver Issues**: Covers requests for refunds or compensation due to issues caused by shoppers or drivers, such as poor communication, unprofessional behavior, or repeated errors.
- **Refund Processing Errors**: Covers requests related to errors or delays in processing refunds, such as discrepancies in refund amounts or unresolved refund requests.
- **Compensation for Service Failures**: Covers requests for compensation due to overall service failures, such as dissatisfaction with the experience, inconvenience caused, or repeated issues.

---

## Payment and Transaction Issues

**Covers issues related to payment processing, declined transactions, incorrect charges, and card-related problems.**

### Subcategories:

- **Insufficient Funds**: Issues where transactions fail due to insufficient funds on the payment card or account.
- **Incorrect Charges**: Cases involving customers or retailers being charged incorrectly, including overcharges, double charges, or charges for unordered items.
- **Card Activation or Assignment Issues**: Problems related to activating, assigning, or linking payment cards to devices or accounts.
- **High Transaction Amounts**: Payment issues caused by transaction amounts exceeding card or system limits.
- **Refund and Reprocessing Issues**: Requests for refunds or issues with reprocessing payments for canceled or incomplete orders.
- **Order Cancellations Due to Payment Issues**: Orders canceled due to payment failures, including insufficient funds or declined transactions.
- **Payment Processing Errors**: Technical issues during payment processing, such as app errors, barcode scanning problems, or system glitches.
- **Unpaid or Missing Payments**: Situations where payments are not recorded, remain unpaid, or are missing from the system despite order completion.

## Shopper and Driver Issues

**Covers complaints or requests related to shopper or driver behavior, performance, or assignment.**

### Subcategories:

- **Shopper Misconduct**: Covers issues related to inappropriate, unprofessional, or fraudulent behavior by shoppers, including theft, improper handling of items, or policy violations.
- **Driver Misconduct**: Covers issues related to inappropriate or unprofessional behavior by drivers, such as misconduct during delivery, unsanitary conditions, or failure to follow instructions.
- **Order Not Delivered**: Covers cases where orders were marked as delivered but were not received by the customer, including potential misdelivery or fraudulent marking of orders.
- **Order Delays**: Covers issues related to delayed deliveries or pickups, including inquiries about estimated delivery times or delays caused by shopper or driver issues.
- **Shopper or Driver Reassignment**: Covers requests to reassign orders to a different shopper or driver due to performance issues, unresponsiveness, or customer dissatisfaction.
- **Improper Item Handling**: Covers issues related to mishandling of items by shoppers or drivers, such as damaged goods, incorrect substitutions, or improper storage.
- **Unauthorized or Fraudulent Activity**: Covers cases of unauthorized actions by shoppers or drivers, such as adding unauthorized items, fraudulent transactions, or account misuse.
- **Customer Complaints About Behavior**: Covers customer-reported complaints about shopper or driver behavior, including rudeness, inappropriate communication, or discomfort caused during interactions.

---

## Technical and System Issues

**Covers technical issues with apps, devices, or systems impacting order processing, staging, or completion.**

### Subcategories:

- **Order Stuck in System**: Covers issues where orders are stuck at any stage in the system, such as shopping, checkout, staging, or delivery, preventing progress or completion.
- **Order Cancellation Issues**: Covers issues related to the inability to cancel orders due to system errors or glitches, including requests for manual cancellation.
- **Device or App Malfunctions**: Covers issues caused by device or app malfunctions, such as freezing, login problems, app crashes, or unresponsive devices.
- **Payment and Refund Issues**: Covers issues related to payment processing, refunds, or payment discrepancies caused by system errors.
- **Order Assignment or Reassignment Issues**: Covers issues where orders need to be assigned or reassigned due to system glitches, unavailable devices, or shopper errors.
- **Caper Cart and Hardware Issues**: Covers issues specifically related to Caper Carts, tablets, or other hardware malfunctions, such as scanning problems, charging issues, or unresponsive screens.
- **Network and Connectivity Issues**: Covers issues caused by network outages, connectivity problems, or communication failures affecting order processing or device functionality.
- **Order Processing Errors**: Covers issues where orders cannot be processed due to system glitches, such as errors during checkout, staging, or label printing.
